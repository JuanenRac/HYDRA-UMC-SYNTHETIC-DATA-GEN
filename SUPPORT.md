# Support Information 🛠️ (HYDRA-UMC-SYNTHETIC-DATA-GEN)

Thank you for using the HYDRA-UMC-SYNTHETIC-DATA-GEN! Here is how you can get help:

## 📺 Video Tutorials & Demos
The best way to understand how to generate training datasets for your components is through our official YouTube channel:
[youtube.com/@electrohobby3d](https://youtube.com/@electrohobby3d)

## ✉️ Direct Technical Inquiries
For industrial licensing, dataset training consulting, or specific technical blockers:
Email: `electrohobby3d@gmail.com`

## 🐛 Bug Reports
If you found a real error in the renderer or annotator scripts, please open a **GitHub Issue** in this repository. 
*Please search existing issues before opening a new one.*

## ❌ What is NOT support?
- We do not provide support for non-YOLO/COCO data formats.
- Please do not use Issues for "How to" questions.
